Our Services

Everything your pet needs to stay happy and healthy.

🩺

Health Checkup

Routine health examinations for your pet.

More details

A general visit can include a review of your pet's health history and a nose-to-tail physical examination. The veterinarian can discuss changes in behavior, answer care questions, and recommend follow-up if needed.

💉

Vaccination

Book appointments for recommended vaccinations.

More details

The veterinarian reviews your pet's age, health, lifestyle, and vaccination history to discuss an appropriate vaccine plan. Bring any available medical records to the appointment.

✂️

Pet Grooming

Bathing, brushing, and grooming services.

More details

Grooming may include bathing, coat brushing, nail trimming, and basic coat care. Tell the groomer about your pet's sensitivities or comfort needs before the visit.

❤️

Pet Wellness

General wellness consultations for your pet.

More details

A wellness consultation focuses on everyday habits such as activity, weight, preventive care, and changes you have noticed. The veterinarian can help identify topics that may need a separate examination.

🦷

Dental Care

Oral health checks and dental cleaning guidance.

More details

The veterinarian can check your pet's mouth and discuss signs of dental problems, home-care routines, and whether a professional cleaning should be considered.

🥗

Nutrition Advice

Food and diet guidance tailored to your pet.

More details

Discuss your pet's current food, feeding routine, age, activity, and any dietary concerns. Recommendations depend on your pet's individual needs and veterinary history.

🩹

First Aid Consultation

Get guidance on basic pet first aid and next steps.

More details

Learn general preparedness and how to recognize when prompt veterinary care may be needed. This consultation is not emergency treatment; contact an emergency veterinarian immediately for urgent symptoms or injuries.

🐾

Puppy & Kitten Care

Helpful care advice for your newest family member.

More details

New-pet visits can cover early health checks, vaccination planning, parasite prevention, feeding, socialization, and routine care. Bring any records from the breeder, shelter, or previous caregiver.
